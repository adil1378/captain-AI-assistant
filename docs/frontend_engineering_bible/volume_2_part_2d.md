# Captain AI OS — Frontend Engineering Bible
## Frontend Volume 2 — Part 2D
### Captain Core Lighting & Visual Effects

* **Version:** 1.0.0
* **Status:** APPROVED SPECIFICATION

---

### Objective
Define the lighting philosophy and visual effects system for the Captain Core. This specification establishes how light, glow, reflections, transparency, and environmental effects contribute to Captain's presence. These effects exist to communicate intelligence and depth—not decoration.

This document defines visual principles only. It does not specify rendering engines, shaders, or implementation details.

---

### Design Philosophy
Light is the primary visual language of Captain. The Captain Core appears illuminated from within, giving the impression of an intelligent energy source rather than an externally lit object. Lighting reinforces Captain's state and presence without overwhelming the interface.

---

### Lighting Architecture & Visual Effects Principles
* **Internal Illumination:** Self-illuminated core originating naturally from internal structure.
* **Controlled Glow:** Soft, controlled, layered, and purposeful glow. Excessive bloom or neon effects are strictly prohibited.
* **Volumetric Depth Perception:** Established through light, shadow, transparency, reflection, blur, and layer separation.
* **Environmental Lighting & Reflections:** Responds to surrounding interface changes and theme context without losing core identity. Reflections are subtle to reinforce material quality and depth.
* **State-Aware Lighting:** Lighting intensity, glow radius, and emphasis dynamically reflect Captain's operational state (`IDLE`, `ATTENTION`, `LISTENING`, `UNDERSTANDING`, `THINKING`, `EXECUTING`, `RESPONDING`, `WAITING`, `NOTIFICATION`, `RECOVERY`).
* **Performance & Accessibility:** Visual effects scale gracefully across hardware. Lighting never obscures text, reduces contrast, or causes visual distraction.

---

### Scope
This specification defines lighting philosophy, glow principles, depth perception, environmental lighting, reflections, transparency, visual focus, state-aware lighting, performance awareness, accessibility, and expansion strategy. It does not define specific colors, shader code, or audio-reactive rendering pipelines.

---

### Deliverable
After approval, every lighting and visual effect associated with the Captain Core must follow these principles.
